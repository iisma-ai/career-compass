# 05 - Competitors and landscape

Researched Nov 2026. Levels: A official docs/meta, B large study, C small, D vendor/blog/marketing, E own experiments.
Method note: vendor pages were fetched directly where possible (what the vendor says = level D for validity claims, A only for factual product specs like price/length as stated by the owner). Prices change; reseller prices are not official. Everything below was true on the day fetched (Nov 2026).

## Question
What do existing career-interest tools offer (length, method, evidence, price), and where can Career Compass credibly differentiate (adaptive length, explanations, "lost user" mode, free text)?

### Cross-cutting evidence
- Interest inventories are not interchangeable: SDS, O*NET IP, ACT IIS and Strong scores correlate highly but high-point codes often differ (Chu et al. 2022, J Career Assessment 30(4), 776-801). (B) [Illinois experts](https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/what-do-interest-inventories-measure-the-convergence-and-content-/)
- Interest inventories reach a 50.8% career-choice hit rate (Hanna & Rounds 2020, Psychological Bulletin), the ceiling any competitor's "match accuracy" should be read against. (A) See note 03.
- Career-intervention meta-analyses (Brown & Ryan Krane; Whiston et al. 2017, JVB 100, 175-184) are reported (secondary summaries) to favour counsellor-led, repeated interventions with written materials, personalised feedback and world-of-work information over self-directed/computer-only; effect on career decision self-efficacy higher. `[UNVERIFIED: secondary only]` [CDAA summary](https://cdaa.imiscloud.com/Web/Blog/Posts/What-is-best-practice-in-careers-education--An-outline-of-the-evidence.aspx)
- No independent validation of CareerExplorer's or Truity's match algorithms was found in this search. Absence of evidence, not evidence of invalidity.

## Gaps / conflicts
- Item counts for Strong (244 vs 291) and Truity/CareerExplorer are not confirmed on official pages. Strong's price is from resellers.
- Competitor "accuracy" claims are vendor-reported and not comparable to peer-reviewed hit rates.
- Spain/EU: no authoritative list of public tests; need a targeted look at SEPE/Educaweb/regional orientation services and EU tools (e.g. Euroguidance/ESCO-based tools).

## Implications: where Career Compass can differentiate (our interpretation)
1. **Adaptive length:** every free competitor uses a fixed form (30-60 items for validated ones, about 30 min for CareerExplorer). A CAT that gets to a stable top-k in about 20-30 items would be shorter than the SDS (250) and Strong (244) with documented precision (see note 02). Must be demonstrated in E4/E5.
2. **Explanations:** vendor pages show scores and lists; explaining each match in terms of the user's own dimension scores and O*NET tasks is a clear gap. Needs user testing.
3. **"Lost user" mode:** none of the tools reviewed advertises a path for people with no idea what they like (concrete-activity items, "don't know", exploration prompts). Hypothesis, no direct evidence in this review.
4. **Free text:** none offers free-text input; risky (see note 04) but differentiating if grounded.
5. **Transparency of evidence:** publish our reliability and agreement numbers, and show uncertainty ("careers worth exploring"). Competitors mostly assert "accurate".
6. Build on O*NET (CC BY 4.0; attribution required) rather than competing on content. Spanish version: Mini-IP is already available in Spanish via Mi Proximo Paso; check whether items can be reused.
7. Position as complement to counsellors: meta-analytic evidence (secondary) says counsellor-led and personalised-feedback interventions work better than self-administered ones, so provide a printable/shareable summary for a counsellor.

## Open questions
Confirm Strong item count and price from CPP; Truity and CareerExplorer test lengths; Spanish public tools (SEPE, Educaweb, regional); independent evaluation of CareerExplorer matches; 16Personalities career validity.
